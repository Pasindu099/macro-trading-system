# P0 source and scale scan — before fixes

- Local production-clone scan: 26,585 non-future feature rows across USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, NZD, CAD and CHF; 397 `(currency,country,indicator)` series. Comparing each print to the prior 12-observation median after at least six prior prints yielded 2,348 ratio alerts in 215 series. Ratio alerts are screening signals, not automatic quarantine decisions; signed flows and percentage changes near zero cause many false positives. No other series was changed.
- Confirmed mixed definition: EU `consumer_inflation_expectation` has 128 releases; 76 actuals have magnitude ≥10. ECB's 12-month median inflation expectation was 3.0% on 18 Sep 2026 ([ECB release](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr260918~295b3ab978.ga.html)). The European Commission's September survey shows 35.2 for the euro-area balance of expected price trends over the next 12 months, released 29 Sep 2026 ([statistical annex, Table 4](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/67757055-585f-4928-a381-35f3f0dd5b97_en?filename=bcs_2026_09_statistical_annex_en.pdf)). Its [2023 table](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/cdeaacc1-47a1-443a-98e3-9e0bd2930c47_en?filename=bcs_2024_06_statistical_annex_en_0.pdf) confirms that low readings 4.8 and 9.0 are also Commission balances. EODHD labels both sources `Consumer Inflation Expectation` without a source or unit field; the local indicator declares `index` even for ECB percent prints. The split uses current-month reference periods or values/previous/estimate ≥10, including holiday-delayed balances.
- EUR headline HICP YoY's 3.8% Sep 2026 print and 3.2% prior match [Eurostat's flash estimate](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-02102026-ap). Its standardized score 4.360333 is a plausible large move, not a unit mismatch: actual 3.8, prior six-observation mean approximately 3.1, small prior standard deviation. Retain it; the ±5 cap will not alter it.
- Additional ratio alerts requiring separate review: JP foreign bond investment (119), JP stock investment by foreigners (136), AU building permits (40), NZ global dairy trade price index (39), DE factory orders MoM (28), CA employment change (18), FR PPI YoY (22). These are volatile signed changes or near-zero-median series; none is proven to be a unit mix by this scan. No automatic repair is proposed for them in P0.
- No source-level unit field exists in the stored EODHD event JSON, so a numeric scan cannot certify every declared unit. The EU percent-versus-balance mismatch was confirmed against ECB and Commission primary sources; other unit metadata require source-by-source review before changing them.
- Future-actual ingestion path: the Oct 4 calendar backfill requested October/November EODHD events. EODHD returned `actual` 0.4/2.7 for Oct 22 Japan CPI and 3.2 for Oct 29 industrial production; retrieval time 2026-10-04 13:40 UTC. `IngestService` accepted the payload without a release-time check, and the Macro State dataset/feature jobs used all non-null actuals. These three rows, not future schedule-only events, advanced the shared score date.
