How to use it: COT is weekly and lags by three days, so it is risk context, not a timing tool. It covers CME and ICE futures, a sample of a market that trades mostly OTC. Extremes can persist for months; the signal is extreme positioning plus price starting to move against it.

1 · Where are the big traders betting?

Hedge funds' bets on each currency, compared with the last {{lookback}}
How to read this Each bar shows how big the hedge-fund bet on a currency is compared with the last {{lookback}}. Far left = the biggest bet against it in that period, far right = the biggest bet on it. A bet at an extreme leaves little room to add more, so a surprise the other way can force a fast unwind. That is a risk to watch, not a signal on its own.
Fast money = hedge funds: borrow to trade, move in and out quickly. Slow money = pension funds and asset managers: long-term, move slowly.
